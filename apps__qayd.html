اشتراكاتك تنسحب كل شهر وإنت ما تدري.

Qayd: All your subscriptions on one page

A light, simplified take on Masarifi. It reads your bank SMS on its own with no bank to pick, shows what you spend and analyses it, with a dedicated page for your subscriptions: what you pay each month and where it goes.
